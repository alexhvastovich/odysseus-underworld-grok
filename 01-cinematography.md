# Cinematography Bible — Odysseus Underworld

Use this document as the **global visual contract** for every shot. Copy the **Global Prompt Block** (below) into each Grok generation, then add shot-specific direction.

---

## Global Prompt Block (paste into every shot)

```
Cinematic 16:9 1080p. Compose for 2.39:1 anamorphic widescreen inside the frame.
35mm film aesthetic: subtle photochemical grain, natural contrast roll-off, no HDR crunch.
Ancient Mediterranean realism — Bronze Age/Iron Age Aegean, not Rome, not high fantasy.
Nolan-adjacent scale and practical realism: real locations, real weather, real firelight.
Muted palette: charcoal black, wet stone grey, cold blue-black shadows, torch amber,
 oxidized bronze, bleached linen, dried blood brown. No neon, no oversaturated teal-orange.
Volumetric smoke and sea mist. Natural skin texture, wind-chapped, sun-weathered.
Costume: hand-woven linen and wool, coarse edges, no pristine costumes.
Shades of the dead: faint, human, partially obscured by smoke — NOT glowing spirits.
**Depth layering:** nearest shades at torchlight edge; **far ranks mass in deep darkness** — small faces, many bodies, unsettling scale. Scary through distance and hunger, not horror-monster makeup.
Environmental audio only unless dialogue is specified: wind, distant surf, cavern drip, fire crackle.
No on-screen text. No logos. No anamorphic lens flare spam.
```

## Lens & camera grammar

| Setup | Lens feel | Use for |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Aerial / establishing | 24–28mm | Geography, scale, dread |
| Wide master | 32–40mm | Full bodies in environment |
| Medium | 50–65mm | Dialogue coverage, walk-and-talk |
| Over-shoulder | 65–85mm | Conversation axis |
| Close-up | 85–100mm | Prophecy lines, reactions |
| Insert | 100mm+ macro | Hands, blood bowl, fire, oar detail |

**Movement discipline:**
- Establishing: slow aerial drift or locked wide
- Dialogue: mostly locked or imperceptible handheld (micro-drift only)
- Reveals: single motivated move — slow push-in or lateral track, never both
- No unmotivated orbit shots. No drone acrobatics after shot 01.

---

## Lighting

**Exterior (shore / descent path):** Pre-dawn or late dusk. Cold ambient skylight + weak torch practicals on Odysseus and crew. Moonless.

**Ritual pit / cavern mouth:** Firelight from below (pit) and side (braziers). Underlighting on faces during ritual. Smoke catches amber light.

**Underworld interior:** Low-key. Cold blue fill from open darkness; warm amber rim from torches/oil lamps. No single hard source from behind camera.

**Shades:** 1–2 stops under key. Soft edges, smoke overlap, no bloom.

---

## Character axis (180° rule)

**Scene geography (lock for Acts II–V):**

```
        [ ABYSS / DARKNESS ]
               ↑
    TIRESIAS ← • → ODYSSEUS
   (camera-right)   (camera-left)
               ↓
         [ RITUAL PIT / FIRE ]
```

- **Odysseus** eyeline: slightly **camera-right** toward Tiresias in dialogue shots.
- **Tiresias** eyeline: slightly **camera-left** toward Odysseus.
- **Neither character looks into the lens** except where explicitly marked (none in this production).
- Reverse shots swap camera side but preserve eyelines above.

---

## Performance notes

**Odysseus:** Late 30s–40s, war-hardened, controlled fear. Speaks with weight, not theatrics. Listens more than he talks in Act IV.

**Tiresias:** Ancient, blind, calm authority. Sightless eyes milky or scarred. Voice low, unhurried — prophecy as memory, not performance.

**Shades:** Slow, drifting movement. No aggressive lunging unless specified (they do not in this cut).

## Duration philosophy

| Length | Feel | Use |
|--------|------|-----|
| 6–7s | Breath, reaction, insert | Emotional beats, cutaways |
| 8–9s | Standard coverage | Walks, medium dialogue |
| 10–12s | Extended beat | Prophecy lines, establishing |
| 15s | Sparingly | Only if dialogue needs air — max 2 shots in this production |

This production totals **242 seconds** across 26 shots — slightly over 4:00 for trim flexibility in stitch.

---

## What to avoid

- Marvel-style quips or modern cadence in dialogue delivery
- Glowing green Underworld clichés
- Greek temple white marble (wrong period/location for this scene)
- Leather armor hero costumes — Odysseus is travel-worn, not parade-ready
- Camera staring contests (characters looking at lens)
- Multiple camera moves in one 10s clip
- Cramming more than ~2 short sentences of dialogue per 10s shot
