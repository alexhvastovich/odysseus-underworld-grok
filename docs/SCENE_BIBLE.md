# Odysseus Underworld — 21 Scene Bible

Scene 1. Night at the edge of the world. A black shore, a beached ship in the mist. No one yet. Only wind and the sea.

Scene 2. The cave has already swallowed him. Odysseus walks deeper into the wet stone, torchlight on the tan of his cloak.

Scene 3. He kneels at a pit in the grey sand. Fire. Bronze. The ritual begins.

Scene 4. His hands pour milk, honey, wine, and blood. The dead can smell it.

Scene 5. He speaks, not to a man, to the dark: "Stay back… until the blood is tasted."

Scene 6. They come anyway. Faces in smoke. A hunger with no bodies.

Scene 7. The crowd thins. A path opens through them.

Scene 8. An old blind man walks toward him, staff in hand. Odysseus does not turn. We see only his back.

Scene 9. Closer. Still no words. The prophet has arrived.

Scene 10. Odysseus: "Tell me what the gods have hidden from me."

Scene 11. Tiresias: "First you must hear what awaits you — and what you must not touch."

Scene 12. "Poseidon still hunts you for the eye of his son."

Scene 13. "On Thrinacia's isle, leave Helios' cattle unharmed — or your ship and your men will perish."

Scene 14. Odysseus: "And if I keep my hands from them?"

Scene 15. "You will reach Ithaca — but find your hall filled with suitors devouring your wealth."

Scene 16. "You must break them. Then take an oar and walk inland… until a stranger calls it a winnowing fan."

Scene 17. "Plant it in the earth. Sacrifice to Poseidon. Then death will come gently, from the sea."

Scene 18. Odysseus: "Is there no other path?"

Scene 19. Tiresias: "There is only the path you make."

Scene 20. Odysseus holds on that. The dark does not answer.

Scene 21. He turns from the pit. The two silent sailors are with him again. They walk out of the cavern mouth, toward the ship, toward the living world. The dead stay behind.
